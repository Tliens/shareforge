FAQ

What sizes do social platforms need for share cards?

X/Twitter posts work best at 1200×675 (16:9), generic Open Graph link cards at 1200×630, WeChat Official Account covers at 900×383 (plus 383×383 for secondary posts), Xiaohongshu/RED at 1242×1656, Instagram at 1080×1080, LinkedIn at 1200×627 and Douyin/Video covers at 1080×1440. All ship as one-click presets here.

Is it free? Do I need an account?

100% free, no signup, no watermark, no export limits.

Are my screenshots uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything renders locally with HTML5 Canvas — your text, screenshots and logo never leave your device, and the page keeps working offline.

Can I export all platform sizes at once?

Yes — type once, click “Export all 8 sizes”, and you get one ready-to-post PNG per platform. Single-size download and copy-to-clipboard are also available.

What format are the exports?

Lossless PNG files at each platform's exact pixel dimensions, ready to upload as-is.
